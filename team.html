About the Founder

The person behind ChainPlus's blockchain and cryptocurrency coverage.

Our Mission

ChainPlus is an independent blockchain and cryptocurrency news aggregator and analysis platform. We combine real-time data from over 300 verified sources with original editorial analysis to help readers understand the fast-moving crypto landscape. Our coverage spans Bitcoin, Ethereum, DeFi, NFTs, regulation, security, and emerging blockchain technology.

Editorial Standards

All content on ChainPlus follows our editorial methodology. We prioritize accuracy, source attribution, and transparency. Our aggregation pipeline filters out low-quality or duplicate content.

Team

Mike Tembo

Founder & Editor-in-Chief

ChainPlus is founded and operated by Mike Tembo, an independent publisher with a background in software engineering and blockchain technology. Mike oversees all editorial decisions, content strategy, and technical development. His hands-on experience includes building data pipelines, market analysis tools, and crypto monitoring systems. All editorial content is reviewed for accuracy, source attribution, and transparency before publication.

Editorial & Research

Content & Analysis

Our editorial coverage combines automated aggregation from over 300 verified sources with original analysis and data-backed research. Each article is tagged with its category, publication date, and author attribution. We publish original investigations, market cycle analysis, on-chain data studies, and practical guides for crypto users.

Manual editorial review: Original Insights articles are written, fact-checked against primary sources, and reviewed for accuracy before publication. Corrections are published promptly when errors are identified.

Editorial standards follow our published methodology.

Engineering & Data

Technology & Infrastructure

Our engineering work includes the news aggregation pipeline, real-time market data integration via CoinGecko, open-source repository tracking via GitHub, topic extraction and sentiment analysis, and the prerendering system that ensures content is visible to search engines and accessible without JavaScript. All tools are built in-house.
