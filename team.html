The people behind ChainPlus's blockchain and cryptocurrency coverage.

Our Mission

ChainPlus is an independent blockchain and cryptocurrency news aggregator and analysis platform. We combine real-time data from over 300 verified sources with original editorial analysis to help readers understand the fast-moving crypto landscape. Our coverage spans Bitcoin, Ethereum, DeFi, NFTs, regulation, security, and emerging blockchain technology.

Editorial Standards

All content on ChainPlus follows our editorial methodology. We prioritize accuracy, source attribution, and transparency. Every article includes credibility scoring based on source reputation, and our aggregation pipeline filters out low-quality or duplicate content.

Editorial Team

Our editorial team aggregates, verifies, and contextualizes blockchain and cryptocurrency news from over 300 verified sources. We apply automated credibility scoring and manual editorial review to ensure accuracy and relevance.

Data & Engineering

Technology Team

Our engineering team builds and maintains the data pipelines, market data integrations, and analysis tools that power ChainPlus. We use CoinGecko for market data, GitHub for open-source tracking, and custom NLP for topic extraction and sentiment analysis.
