Research Standards

Every ChainPlus Research article follows a consistent structure:

  • Executive summary — Key findings in 2-3 sentences.
  • Charts & data tables — Visual and tabular data with accessible fallbacks.
  • Methodology — Data sources, sampling, and analysis methods.
  • Key observations — Numbered findings with evidence.
  • Limitations — What the data cannot tell us.
  • Related research — Links to connected analysis.
  • Disclaimer — Not financial advice. Data sourced from public APIs.

All research is conducted by Mike Tembo using publicly available data. No proprietary or paid data sources are used. All figures are reproducible from the cited APIs.
