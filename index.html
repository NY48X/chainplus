About ChainPlus

ChainPlus is a real-time cryptocurrency news aggregator and market data platform. We collect headlines and short excerpts from 300+ trusted crypto news sources, track market prices for 100+ cryptocurrencies, surface trending topics from our news feed with sentiment signals, and publish original data-backed research and editorial insights.

Our goal is to cut through the noise in crypto information. The crypto space generates thousands of headlines daily, most of which are price commentary or recycled press releases. ChainPlus filters, categorizes, and presents this information so you can find what matters quickly — with direct links to the original publishers.

What We Offer

  • Featured Analysis — Original, data-backed research with methodology and reproducible sources. See Research.
  • Market Snapshot — Static market context on the homepage and live prices, 24h changes, and charts on the Markets page.
  • Breaking News — Live headlines and short excerpts from 300+ sources, updated every 45 seconds, always linking to the original source.
  • Topics — Trending topics from the news feed with sentiment analysis.
  • Open Source — Trending blockchain repositories from GitHub.
  • Insights — Original editorial explainers, guides, and analysis written by Mike Tembo.

Data Sources

News from 300+ RSS/Atom feeds. Market data from CoinGecko. Open-source data from GitHub. Sentiment from the Fear & Greed Index. All data is attributed to its original source.

Editorial Standards

We do not republish full articles — only headlines and short excerpts with links to original sources. Sponsored posts are accepted and clearly labeled as such. All original research and insights are written by Mike Tembo based on independent analysis of public data.
