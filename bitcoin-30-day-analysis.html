Bitcoin 30-Day Price Analysis: Data, Trends and What the Charts Show

By Mike Tembo · November 5, 2026 · Data Analysis

Overview

This is an original ChainPlus data analysis of Bitcoin (BTC) price action over the past 30 days. We pulled hourly price data from CoinGecko, downsampled to daily observations, and analyzed the trend, volatility, and key inflection points. Data was collected on November 5, 2026. All prices are in USD.

Key finding: Bitcoin declined approximately 8.5% over the 30-day period, from $86,592 to $79,226. The decline was not linear — it featured two distinct sell-off phases separated by a brief recovery, suggesting institutional rebalancing rather than panic selling.

Data & Reproducibility

  • Data collection period: October 6, 2026 – November 5, 2026 (30 days).
  • API endpoint: CoinGecko /coins/bitcoin/market_chart?vs_currency=usd&days=30
  • Raw data points: 721 hourly observations.
  • Downsampling: First observation of each 24-hour window (30 daily points).
  • Calculation formula: Daily change % = (Price_dayN − Price_day1) ÷ Price_day1 × 100. All percentage changes are relative to Day 1 price ($86,592).
  • Reproduction: Run curl "https://api.coingecko.com/api/v3/coins/bitcoin/market_chart?vs_currency=usd&days=30" to fetch the same dataset. CSV download: bitcoin-30d-prices.csv.
  • Limitations: This analysis uses price data only. It does not incorporate volume, on-chain metrics, or macroeconomic factors. Price attribution to "institutional rebalancing" is speculative and based on curve shape analysis only. No volume data is included.

Methodology

  • Data source: CoinGecko public API (/coins/bitcoin/market_chart), 30-day range, USD denomination.
  • Sampling: 721 hourly data points downsampled to 30 daily observations (first point of each 24-hour window).
  • Analysis: Simple trend identification, percentage change calculation, and visual inspection of price curve shape.
  • Limitations: This analysis uses price data only. It does not incorporate volume, on-chain metrics, or macroeconomic factors. Past performance is not indicative of future results.

Key Observations

  1. Two-phase decline: The first 10 days saw a gradual 4% drop from $86,592 to ~$83,100. Days 10-18 featured a sharper 6% decline to ~$78,100. Days 18-30 showed consolidation around $79,000.
  2. No panic selling: The decline was orderly with no single-day drops exceeding 3%. This suggests institutional rebalancing rather than retail panic.
  3. Support level: Price found support near $77,000-$78,000 in the final week, forming what appears to be a consolidation range.
  4. Volume context: Without volume data in this analysis, we cannot determine whether the decline was accompanied by high or low trading activity. We recommend cross-referencing with exchange flow data.

What This Means

The 30-day picture shows Bitcoin in a correction phase after a period of higher prices. The orderly nature of the decline and the formation of a support range suggest this is a consolidation, not a crash. However, without volume and on-chain data, we cannot determine whether accumulation or distribution is occurring at these levels.

We recommend monitoring exchange inflows/outflows and whale wallet activity over the next 7-14 days to determine whether the $77,000-$78,000 support level will hold or break.
