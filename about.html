About ChainPlus

ChainPlus is a real-time news aggregator for the blockchain and cryptocurrency ecosystem. We collect headlines and short excerpts from hundreds of independent publishers and link directly to the original reporting.

What we do

We monitor over 300 verified RSS and Atom feeds covering Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, DeFi, NFTs, regulation, markets and more. New items are fetched and ranked by recency, then displayed in a fast, readable interface with market context and sentiment signals.

What we do not do

  • We do not republish full articles. Only headlines and short excerpts are shown.
  • We do not provide financial advice. Nothing on this site should be treated as an investment recommendation.
  • We are not affiliated with the publishers we link to.

Our Team

ChainPlus is built and maintained by Mike Tembo, an independent publisher with a background in software engineering and blockchain technology. What started as a personal tool for tracking crypto news evolved into a full platform combining real-time news aggregation from over 300 verified sources, market data, open-source repository tracking, and original data-backed analysis.

We are not affiliated with any exchange, protocol, or token project. We do not accept payment for coverage or promotion. All content is editorially independent.

Our approach combines real-time aggregation from over 300 verified sources with original data-backed analysis. Our Insights section provides explainers, market cycle analysis, on-chain data studies, and practical guides for crypto users.

We believe in transparency, open source, and the power of decentralized systems. Our Insights section contains original analysis written by Mike Tembo. Our news section aggregates headlines and short excerpts from hundreds of independent publishers, with direct links to the original reporting. We don't accept sponsored content or paid promotions.

Editorial Standards

  • Originality: Our Insights articles are original analysis written by Mike Tembo. Our news section aggregates headlines and short excerpts from public RSS feeds, with links to the original sources. We do not republish full articles.
  • Accuracy: We verify facts from multiple sources before publishing analysis. If we make an error, we correct it promptly.
  • Transparency: We disclose our methodology, data sources, and limitations. Our market data comes from CoinGecko, our news from public RSS feeds, and our open-source data from GitHub's public API.
  • Independence: We are not affiliated with any exchange, protocol, or token project. We do not accept payment for coverage or promotion.

Data Sources

  • News: 300+ RSS and Atom feeds from trusted crypto publishers worldwide.
  • Market Data: CoinGecko API — real-time prices, market caps, and 7-day sparklines for 100+ cryptocurrencies.
  • Open Source: GitHub API — trending blockchain repositories sorted by community engagement.
  • Sentiment: Fear & Greed Index — composite sentiment indicator updated daily.
