Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026. This policy explains what cookies and similar technologies ChainPlus uses, why, and how you can control them.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites operate, remember preferences, understand usage and support advertising services.

ChainPlus uses cookies and similar technologies for essential website functionality, analytics and advertising, subject to your consent and applicable legal requirements.

ChainPlus uses Cookiebot, a Google-certified Consent Management Platform (CMP), to obtain and manage your consent. Cookiebot controls which scripts and cookies are loaded based on your choices.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

2.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are required for ChainPlus to function properly. They support essential features such as security, session management, consent preferences and website functionality.

These cookies cannot be disabled through our cookie preference settings.

2.2 Analytics Cookies

Analytics technologies help us understand how visitors use ChainPlus, including page views, traffic sources, engagement and website performance.

Analytics cookies and similar technologies are disabled by default and are only activated after you provide consent.

2.3 Advertising Cookies

ChainPlus may use Google AdSense and other advertising partners to display advertisements.

Advertising technologies may collect information such as device identifiers, browser information, approximate location and browsing activity to deliver and measure advertisements.

Advertising scripts, advertising requests and non-essential advertising cookies are disabled by default and are only activated in accordance with your consent and applicable regional requirements.

Where permitted and appropriately configured, advertising partners may provide non-personalized advertisements. Non-personalized advertising does not use personal browsing activity to select advertisements, although limited data may still be processed for advertising delivery, measurement, frequency capping and fraud prevention.

2.4 Personalization Cookies

These technologies allow advertising partners to personalize advertisements based on information such as browsing activity and interests.

They are only activated when the visitor has provided the required consent.

2.5 Third-party cookies from embedded content

When you select a non-English language, the Google Translate service may set cookies. These are governed by Google's privacy policy.

3. Your Cookie Choices

When you first visit ChainPlus, you can:

  • Accept all optional cookies and technologies.
  • Reject optional cookies and technologies.
  • Manage preferences — independently choose analytics and advertising.

Strictly necessary cookies remain active because they are required for website functionality. Analytics and advertising technologies remain disabled until the relevant consent is provided.

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time through the Cookie Settings link available on ChainPlus. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that occurred before consent was withdrawn.

4. Third-Party Advertising

ChainPlus may work with third-party advertising providers, including Google AdSense. These providers may use their own cookies and similar technologies, subject to their respective privacy policies and applicable consent requirements. We do not control every aspect of third-party data processing. Visitors should review the privacy policies of the relevant providers for additional information.

5. Managing Cookies

You can manage your cookie preferences through the Cookie Settings option on our website. You can also configure your browser to block or delete cookies. However, blocking strictly necessary cookies may affect certain website features.

6. Policy Updates

We may update this Cookie Policy to reflect changes in our website, advertising partners, technologies or applicable legal requirements. The latest version will always be published on this page.

7. Contact

For questions about this Cookie Policy or how ChainPlus uses cookies, contact us through our website's Contact page. See also our Privacy Policy.
