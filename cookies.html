Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026. This policy explains what cookies and similar technologies ChainPlus uses, why, and how you can control them.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, improve user experience, and provide information to site owners. Similar technologies include web beacons, pixel tags, and local storage.

3. How to manage cookies

You can control cookies in several ways:

  • Cookie banner — When you first visit the Site, a banner asks for your consent. Advertising cookies are only set after you click "Accept".
  • Browser settings — Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. See your browser's help documentation.
  • Google Ads Settings — Opt out of personalized advertising at google.com/settings/ads.
  • Network Advertising Initiative — Opt out at optout.networkadvertising.org.

Note: Blocking essential cookies may affect site functionality (theme, language preference).

4. Do Not Track

Our Site does not currently respond to "Do Not Track" browser signals, as there is no industry standard for handling them. We honor cookie consent choices made through our banner.
