Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026. This policy explains what cookies and similar technologies ChainPlus uses, why, and how you can control them.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, improve user experience, and provide information to site owners. Similar technologies include web beacons, pixel tags, and local storage.

2. How we use cookies

ChainPlus uses cookies and local storage for the following purposes:

These are required for the Site to function and cannot be disabled.

2.2 Advertising cookies

These may be set by advertising partners to serve and measure advertisements. They are not set before consent; they are only set after you grant consent via the cookie banner. When consent is denied, AdSense serves non-personalized ads and no advertising cookies are placed. You can manage or disable them through your browser settings.

The Google AdSense script loads on every page to serve advertisements. When consent is granted, personalized ads may be served and advertising cookies (such as __gads, __gpi, NID, IDE) are set. When consent is denied or not yet given, AdSense serves non-personalized ads only and no advertising cookies are placed. Analytics tracking (Google Tag Manager / GA4) is completely disabled until consent is granted. For a complete list of cookies set by Google, see Google's Cookie Policy.

2.3 Third-party cookies from embedded content

When you select a non-English language, the Google Translate service may set cookies. These are governed by Google's privacy policy.

3. How to manage cookies

You can control cookies in several ways:

  • Cookie consent (Cookiebot) — We use Cookiebot, a Google-certified Consent Management Platform (CMP), to obtain and manage your cookie consent. When you first visit the Site, a banner asks for your consent before setting any non-essential cookies. Advertising and analytics cookies are not set until you grant consent. If you reject, AdSense serves non-personalized ads and only essential cookies (theme, language) are used. Analytics tracking is disabled. You can change your choice at any time via the Cookie Settings link in the footer or by clicking the Cookiebot icon.
  • Browser settings — Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. See your browser's help documentation.
  • Google Ads Settings — Opt out of personalized advertising at google.com/settings/ads.
  • Network Advertising Initiative — Opt out at optout.networkadvertising.org.

Note: Blocking essential cookies may affect site functionality (theme, language preference).

4. Do Not Track

Our Site does not currently respond to "Do Not Track" browser signals, as there is no industry standard for handling them. We honor cookie consent choices made through our banner.

5. Updates to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Questions about cookies: support@chainplus.tech or via our contact page.
