Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026. This policy explains how ChainPlus ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use chainplus.tech (the "Site").

1. Who we are

ChainPlus is a real-time cryptocurrency and blockchain news aggregator operated as an independent project. We are the data controller for the personal information described in this policy. For privacy questions or requests, contact us at support@chainplus.tech.

2. Information we collect

We collect minimal information. The Site does not require registration or user accounts.

2.1 Information you provide voluntarily

  • Email address — only if you subscribe to our newsletter or use the contact form. Stored in Cloudflare KV and used solely to send you the requested communications.
  • Name and message — only if you submit the contact form. Used to respond to your inquiry.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Server and request data — IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URL, and request timestamps. These are processed by our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) and Cloudflare (CDN/proxy) as part of standard web infrastructure.
  • Local storage data — We store the following in your browser's localStorage and sessionStorage: language preference (cp-lang), theme preference (cp-theme), cookie consent status (cp-cookies), API base URL (cp-api-base), and cached news/market data (cp-news-cache, cp-markets-cache). This data never leaves your browser.

2.3 Information collected by third parties

  • Advertising partners — Displays advertisements. May collect device information, browsing behavior, and IP address to serve relevant ads. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare — Provides CDN, DNS, and security services. May log IP addresses and request metadata. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Translate — When you select a non-English language, text is sent to Google's translation service. See Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Cookies and tracking technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies. For a detailed breakdown of every cookie we use, its purpose, duration, and provider, see our Cookie Policy.

In summary:

  • Essential cookies — Required for the site to function (theme, language, cookie consent). Cannot be disabled.
  • Advertising cookies — Set by advertising partners to serve and measure ads. These may be set on page load. You can manage them through your browser settings or our cookie banner.
  • No analytics cookies — We do not use Google Analytics or similar tracking tools.

5. Third-party services and data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the following service providers, strictly for the purposes described:

  • Advertising partners — Advertising. Receives device and browsing data for ad personalization. Learn more.
  • Cloudflare — CDN, DNS, security. Processes request metadata. Learn more.
  • Resend — Email delivery for newsletter and contact form responses. Receives subscriber email addresses. Learn more.
  • GitHub Pages — Static hosting. Processes request metadata. Learn more.

We may also disclose information if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of ChainPlus or others.

6. Data retention

  • Newsletter subscribers — Retained until you unsubscribe. You can request deletion at any time by emailing support@chainplus.tech.
  • Contact form submissions — Retained for up to 12 months, then deleted.
  • Cached data in your browser — Controlled by you. Clear your browser data to remove.
  • Server logs — Retained by hosting providers per their own policies (typically 30–90 days).

7. Data security

We use HTTPS (TLS 1.2+) for all connections. The site is served via Cloudflare's CDN with DDoS protection and bot management. Cloudflare Workers handle API requests with rate limiting. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. International data transfers

ChainPlus is hosted on GitHub Pages (United States) and uses Cloudflare (global). If you access the Site from outside the United States, your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States. We rely on standard contractual clauses and the service providers' compliance programs for lawful transfers.

9. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access — Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification — Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Erasure — Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Portability — Request your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Objection — Object to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Withdraw consent — Withdraw consent for newsletter or advertising at any time.
  • Opt out of sale/sharing — We do not sell personal information. You can opt out of personalized advertising via Google Ads Settings.

To exercise any of these rights, email support@chainplus.tech. We will respond within 30 days. We may need to verify your identity before processing your request.

10. California residents (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, the right to delete it, the right to correct it, and the right to opt out of its sale or sharing. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. To submit a request, email support@chainplus.tech.

11. European Economic Area and UK residents (GDPR/UK GDPR)

If you are in the EEA or UK, you have the rights described in Section 9. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. Our legal basis for processing is set out in Section 3. We do not use automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal effects.

12. Children's privacy

ChainPlus is not directed at children under 13 (or 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at support@chainplus.tech and we will delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

14. Contact

Questions or concerns about this privacy policy: support@chainplus.tech or via our contact page.
