How Rising Topics Work

Our rising topics engine analyzes headlines from over 300 verified crypto news sources in real time. It extracts meaningful keywords and phrases, filters out common stop words, and ranks topics by frequency of mention. Each topic is classified with a sentiment indicator based on associated keywords.

Topics are refreshed every 60 seconds as new headlines are published. The "Pulse" section shows the top topics gaining traction right now. "All Rising" lists every topic we're tracking, ranked by mentions. "Just Emerged" highlights topics that appeared in headlines for the first time recently.

Click any topic to see all related headlines. Use the sector filters to explore topics by category — DeFi, Layer 1, NFT, regulation, and more.

About Rising Topics

Rising Topics is ChainPlus's topic discovery dashboard. It analyzes hundreds of crypto news headlines in real time to identify which topics are gaining traction, how sentiment is shifting, and which stories are emerging before they hit mainstream coverage.

Unlike simple keyword counting, our trending engine uses bigram extraction (two-word phrases) to catch multi-word entities like "Pump.fun" or "ETF approval." It applies crypto-specific sentiment analysis, frequency thresholds, and daily rotating seeds to surface meaningful trends rather than noise.

How Topics Are Ranked

Topics are ranked by mention frequency across our 300+ news sources. Single words need 2+ mentions to qualify; two-word phrases need 1+ mention. This balances sensitivity (catching emerging topics early) against noise (filtering out one-off mentions).

Sentiment Classification

Each topic is classified as bullish, bearish, or neutral based on surrounding context. We use crypto-specific keyword matching (e.g., "surge" → bullish, "crash" → bearish) with word-boundary matching to reduce false positives. Sentiment is approximate — natural language is messy, and sarcasm or context-dependent usage can trip up keyword-based systems.

Topic Sectors

Topics are grouped into sectors: Layer 1 (blockchain platforms), DeFi (decentralized finance), Security (hacks, exploits, audits), Markets (price movements, trading), NFT (non-fungible tokens), and Regulation (policy, SEC, compliance). This helps you quickly find topics relevant to your interests.
