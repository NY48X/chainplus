DeFi Protocol Liquidity Analysis: TVL, Volume, and Efficiency Metrics

By Mike Tembo · November 5, 2026 · Data Analysis

Overview

This is an original ChainPlus analysis comparing liquidity metrics across five major DeFi protocols: Uniswap V3, Curve, Aave, Compound, and MakerDAO. We examined Total Value Locked (TVL), 24-hour trading volume, capital efficiency ratios, and fee revenue. Data was collected from DefiLlama and protocol-specific APIs on November 5, 2026.

Key finding: Uniswap V3 leads in capital efficiency (volume/TVL ratio of 0.42) despite having lower TVL than Aave. Curve dominates stablecoin liquidity with $4.2B TVL but the lowest capital efficiency (0.08). Aave generates the highest fee revenue per dollar of TVL.

Methodology

  • Data source: DefiLlama API (/protocols, /tvl/{protocol}, /summary/fees/{protocol}) + protocol-specific subgraphs. Snapshot: November 5, 2026, 00:00 UTC.
  • Protocols analyzed: Uniswap V3, Curve, Aave V3, Compound V3, MakerDAO (5 largest by TVL on Ethereum mainnet).
  • Metrics: TVL (total value locked), 24h trading volume, capital efficiency (volume/TVL ratio), fee revenue per dollar of TVL, 7d TVL change.
  • Calculation formulas: Volume-to-TVL ratio = 24h volume ÷ TVL. Fee yield = annualized fees ÷ TVL. TVL change % = (TVL_now − TVL_7d_ago) ÷ TVL_7d_ago.
  • Important caveat: Volume-to-TVL ratio is not a direct measure of liquidity efficiency. It indicates how much trading activity occurs relative to locked value, but does not account for liquidity depth, slippage, or impermanent loss.
  • Limitations: TVL can be inflated by recursive lending and double-counting. Volume data includes wash trading on some DEXs. Fee revenue estimates use protocol-reported figures, which may include non-fee revenue. Data is a single-point snapshot, not a time series.

Key Observations

  1. Uniswap V3 leads in capital efficiency: Despite having lower TVL ($3.8B) than Aave ($12.1B) and MakerDAO ($5.8B), Uniswap V3 processes $1.6B in daily volume, giving it a capital efficiency ratio of 0.42 — 6x higher than Aave (0.07) and 60x higher than MakerDAO (0.007).
  2. Curve dominates stablecoin liquidity: With $4.2B TVL, Curve is the largest stablecoin DEX. However, its capital efficiency is only 0.08, meaning most of its locked capital is idle at any given time. This is by design (concentrated liquidity for stable pairs).
  3. Aave generates highest fee revenue: At $0.0003 per dollar of TVL per day, Aave generates more fee revenue per unit of locked capital than any other analyzed protocol. This reflects its lending model (interest rates) vs. DEX models (trading fees).
  4. MakerDAO is least efficient: With only $40M in daily volume against $5.8B TVL (efficiency 0.007), MakerDAO's DAI stablecoin sees relatively little secondary market trading. Most DAI is held or used in other protocols rather than actively traded.
  5. Concentration risk: The top 5 protocols account for over 60% of total DeFi TVL. A smart contract vulnerability in any of these protocols could have cascading effects across the entire DeFi ecosystem.

What This Means

Capital efficiency varies dramatically across DeFi protocols. DEXs like Uniswap V3 achieve high efficiency through concentrated liquidity, while lending protocols like Aave generate revenue through interest rates rather than trading volume. This means "TVL" alone is a misleading metric — a protocol with $12B TVL and 0.07 efficiency is not necessarily "better" than one with $3.8B TVL and 0.42 efficiency.

For users, the key takeaway is: when providing liquidity, consider both the TVL (depth of the pool) and the volume (how often it's used). High TVL with low volume means your capital is sitting idle. High volume with lower TVL means better returns but higher impermanent loss risk.

We recommend monitoring capital efficiency ratios alongside TVL when evaluating DeFi protocols, and being aware of the different revenue models (trading fees vs. interest rates) when comparing protocols.
