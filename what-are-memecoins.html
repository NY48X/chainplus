What Are Memecoins and Why Do They Matter?

By ChainPlus Team · October 17, 2026 · Explainer

Memecoins are cryptocurrencies created as jokes or memes that have gained real market value. Dogecoin, created in 2013 as a joke based on the "Doge" meme, is the most famous example. Shiba Inu, Pepe, and thousands of tokens launched on platforms like pump.fun are others.

Why Do Memecoins Exist?

Memecoins serve a unique function in crypto: they are pure vehicles for speculation and community. Unlike Bitcoin (store of value) or Ethereum (smart contract platform), memecoins have no underlying technology or utility. Their value comes entirely from attention, narrative, and community sentiment.

This sounds absurd, but it reveals something important about markets: value is subjective. A memecoin is worth what people are willing to pay for it. If enough people believe in a meme, the token representing it can achieve a multi-billion dollar market cap.

The pump.fun Phenomenon

pump.fun is a Solana-based platform that allows anyone to create a new token for a few dollars. Since its launch, millions of tokens have been created. Most are worthless within hours. A few achieve viral status and reach millions in market cap.

The platform has democratized token creation — you don't need technical skills or capital to launch a token. But this also means the space is flooded with scams, rug pulls, and abandoned projects. For every successful memecoin, thousands fail.

What Memecoins Tell Us About Markets

Memecoins are a real-time proxy for retail risk appetite. When memecoin trading volume spikes, it signals that retail participants are feeling speculative. Historically, this has preceded both significant rallies (when accompanied by positive catalysts) and sharp corrections (when leverage is overextended).

Memecoins also demonstrate the power of community in crypto. A strong community can sustain a token's value even without fundamentals. Dogecoin has survived for over a decade primarily because of its community and cultural significance.

The Risks

Memecoins are extremely risky. Most lose 90%+ of their value within days or weeks. Rug pulls (where creators drain liquidity and abandon the project) are common. The volatility is extreme — a memecoin can go from $0 to $1 billion and back to $0 in days.

If you participate in memecoin trading, size your positions accordingly. Never invest more than you can afford to lose entirely. Treat memecoins as entertainment with financial stakes, not as investments.

The Bigger Picture

Memecoins are a feature of crypto markets, not a bug. They represent the permissionless, open nature of blockchain technology — anyone can create a token, and the market decides its value. Whether this is a good thing depends on your perspective.

For crypto as a whole, memecoins drive attention and new users to the space. Many people's first crypto experience is buying a memecoin. Some of those users go on to learn about Bitcoin, DeFi, and other more substantive aspects of the ecosystem.

The Economics of Memecoins

Memecoins have unusual economic properties. They have no cash flows, no revenue, and no underlying value. Their price is determined entirely by supply and demand, which is driven by attention and sentiment.

This makes memecoins extremely volatile. A memecoin can increase 1000% in a day and lose 99% the next. This volatility attracts speculators but also creates significant risk.

The total market cap of all memecoins combined exceeds $50 billion at times. This represents real money flowing into assets with no fundamental value. Whether this is a bubble or a new asset class is debated.

Memecoins and Culture

Memecoins are deeply tied to internet culture. They spread through social media, memes, and community engagement. A memecoin's success depends on its ability to capture and sustain attention.

This makes memecoins unpredictable. A meme that goes viral can drive a token to billions in market cap. A meme that fades can crash a token to zero. There's no formula for predicting which memes will resonate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are memecoins a good investment?

Memecoins are extremely risky and most lose value rapidly. They should be treated as speculative entertainment, not investments. Never invest more than you can afford to lose entirely.

How do I buy memecoins?

Memecoins are typically traded on decentralized exchanges (DEXs) like Raydium (Solana) or Uniswap (Ethereum). You'll need a compatible wallet and some SOL or ETH for transaction fees. Be extremely cautious — most memecoins are scams or fail quickly.

Why do memecoins have value?

Memecoins have value because people are willing to buy them. Their value is driven by attention, community, and speculation. There's no underlying cash flow or utility. This makes them fundamentally different from assets like Bitcoin or Ethereum.

What is pump.fun?

pump.fun is a Solana-based platform that allows anyone to create and trade new tokens. It has democratized token creation but also led to an explosion of scams and failed projects. Use with extreme caution.
