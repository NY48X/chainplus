Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly do you respond?

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 48 hours. For urgent matters, please include "URGENT" in your subject line.

Can I request a feature?

Yes. We welcome feature requests and feedback. Please describe the feature you'd like and how it would improve your experience. We review all suggestions and prioritize based on user demand and feasibility.

How do I report a bug?

Please describe the issue, the page where it occurred, your browser and device, and any error messages you saw. Screenshots are helpful. We investigate all bug reports and fix confirmed issues promptly.

Can I submit a news tip?

Yes. If you have a news tip or story idea, please send it to support@chainplus.tech with "News Tip" in the subject line. We review all submissions but cannot guarantee coverage of every tip.
