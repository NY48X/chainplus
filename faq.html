Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about ChainPlus.

Is ChainPlus free?

Yes. ChainPlus is free to read. We monetize through advertising.

How often is the news updated?

The feed refreshes live every 45 seconds — new headlines flow to the top without a full page refresh — and immediately on load. Breaking headlines from the last 2 hours scroll across the top.

Where does the news come from?

Over 300 independent publishers via RSS and Atom feeds. See our methodology.

Do you republish full articles?

No. We show headlines and short excerpts, always linking to the original source.

Are the prices real-time?

Prices come from CoinGecko and refresh on page load. They are indicative, not trade-execution prices.

Is this financial advice?

No. Nothing on ChainPlus is financial advice. Do your own research.

How do I report an issue or request a takedown?

Use the contact form.

About Our Content

How do you ensure accuracy?

We aggregate headlines from trusted publishers and link directly to original sources. Our AI summaries extract key facts from article text but should not replace reading the full article. For market data, we use CoinGecko's API. For open-source data, we use GitHub's public API. We verify all data sources and update our feeds regularly.

Do you accept sponsored content?

No. We do not accept payment for coverage, promotion, or placement. All content on ChainPlus is editorially independent. We do not participate in affiliate programs. Advertising, if displayed, is served automatically by Google AdSense and is clearly distinguished from editorial content.

How do you handle corrections?

If you find an error in our content, please contact us at support@chainplus.tech. We review all correction requests and update content promptly when errors are confirmed. For third-party content (news headlines), corrections should be directed to the original publisher.

What is your editorial policy?

Our editorial policy prioritizes accuracy, transparency, and independence. We aggregate news from 300+ trusted sources and provide AI-generated summaries for convenience. Our original insights are based on our own analysis of on-chain data and market patterns. We do not republish full articles — only headlines and short excerpts with links to original sources. We disclose our data sources, methodology, and limitations on our Methodology page.

Is ChainPlus free to use?

Yes. ChainPlus is completely free to use. We do not charge for access to any content or tools. The site is supported by advertising (Google AdSense) and does not require registration or payment.
